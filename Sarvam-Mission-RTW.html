01
What is Sarvam?

If you were explaining Sarvam to someone conversationally, what would you say?

Sarvam is India's sovereign AI platform, built to understand how India speaks, writes, thinks and reasons.

Think of it this way

The Indian Constitution was written by Indians, for the people of India. Sarvam is essentially that, but for AI.

It runs on compute hosted in India, is powered by frontier-class models of up to 105 billion parameters trained from scratch, and is built by people who understand India's pulse: our languages, the way we mix Hindi and English, and the real problems our banks, insurers and governments are solving. The only difference? Anyone in the world can use it, because Sarvam has open-sourced its flagship models.

Owns every layer: India-hosted compute (token factory), models trained on India-specific data from scratch, and products handling billions of interactions.

Why it's hard to copy

Most Indian AI players focus on one piece. BharatGen and Soket are building models; Gnani is focused on voice AI. To catch up, they'd need India-hosted infrastructure and enterprise-ready products too, all working together in production.

The proof

Selected under the IndiaAI Mission, a five-year defence agreement via its Chanakya division, and MoUs with Tamil Nadu and Odisha.

Everything is built, hosted and governed in India, which is non-negotiable for defence and government work. Deep integrations with these customers mean very low churn risk.
